Mì Quảng in Da Nang: The Authentic Local Dish to Try First

Published 4 November 2026

Mì Quảng: The Signature Dish of Da Nang

Mì Quảng is the defining dish of the Quảng Nam–Đà Nẵng region. It features rice noodles served with shrimp, pork, a hard-boiled egg, roasted peanuts, and fresh herbs. A small amount of rich, turmeric-spiced broth tops the bowl. This dish is widely recognized as a local staple and is deeply rooted in Central Vietnamese tradition.

The flavor profile is bold, spicy, and savory—characteristic of Central Vietnam. The chili peppers (ớt) used are strong even when small, so visitors should prepare for a fiery experience. Mì Quảng is best enjoyed in local eateries, especially those serving the traditional Quang Nam-style version with chicken, quail eggs, and snakehead fish.

Bánh Tráng Cuốn Thị Heo – A Street Food Staple

Bánh tráng cuốn thịt heo (grilled rice paper with pork) is a staple of Da Nang’s street food culture. Thin, grilled rice paper rolls are filled with boiled pork, fresh vegetables, green mango, and a local dipping sauce. This dish is widely available and popular among locals and tourists alike.

It is often found in night markets and small roadside stalls.

Bê Thui Cầu Mống – A Regional Delicacy

Bê thui Cầu Mống originates from Cầu Mống village in Điện Bàn district. This slow-roasted veal dish has golden skin and is typically served with fresh rice paper, herbs, and mắm nem (fermented anchovy sauce). It is a regional delicacy that reflects Da Nang’s rural culinary roots.

This dish is best experienced in local eateries that serve traditional Central Vietnamese fare. It pairs well with a side of rice and is ideal for those who enjoy rich, savory flavors with a strong umami depth.

Bún Mắm – A Bold, Local Favorite

Bún mắm Bé Hà at 130 Bùi Hữu Nghĩa offers a strong fish sauce rice vermicelli dish. The aroma of fish sauce is noticeable from afar. The rice crackers are highly recommended. This dish is not for the weak of palate and is a great choice for foreign tourists seeking authentic local flavors.

It is served in a casual setting and is widely appreciated for its bold taste and traditional preparation.
